ProperPDF vs PDF Expert on iPhone and iPad

PDF Expert is the fuller editor. ProperPDF is the cheaper, offline, cross-platform toolkit.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

PDF Expert is the better editor: it edits text, converts to Office formats and annotates well, and reading, annotating, filling and signing are free. Its merge, compress and convert tools are Premium at $79.99 a year. ProperPDF merges, splits and scans for free, sells Pro for $19.99 a year or $29.99 once for lifetime access (US prices), has no account, and also runs on Android. PDF Expert has no Android app.

Choose PDF Expert if

You annotate a lot, need to change text in PDFs, convert to Word, or work across iPhone, iPad and Mac. It's an Editors' Choice app with a 4.7 rating from over 200,000 App Store ratings.

Choose ProperPDF if

Your work is mostly merging, splitting, scanning, compressing and signing, you also use Android, or you'd rather pay once than every year.

Questions

Is PDF Expert free?

Reading, annotating, and filling and signing forms are free. Editing, merging, compressing, converting and AI are part of Premium.

Does PDF Expert work on Android?

No. It's available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. ProperPDF is on iPhone, iPad and Android.
