Merge PDFs on iPhone and Android without uploading

Pick the files, set the order, save one PDF. Nothing leaves your phone.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

To merge PDFs on your phone without uploading them, open Merge PDF in ProperPDF, pick two or more files, drag them into the order you want and save. The app joins them on the device, so it works offline and you don't need an account. Merging is free on iPhone, iPad and Android. Free exports carry a ProperPDF watermark, which Pro removes.

How to merge PDFs in ProperPDF

  1. Open ProperPDF and tap Merge PDF.
  2. Select two or more PDFs.
  3. Long-press a file and drag it to change the merge order.
  4. Save. The merged PDF appears in your library and the original files stay as they were.
  5. Open the result to check it, then share it from the share sheet.

When merging helps

A contract with its attachments, a visa application with its supporting documents, a month of receipts: one PDF is easier to send and easier to find later than five separate files.

Merging copies the pages as they are. It doesn't re-compress them, so text and scans look the same as in the originals.

After you merge

Use Organize Pages to reorder, rotate or delete pages in the merged file. If it's too big for email, Compress PDF (Pro) saves a smaller copy.

Questions

Is merging PDFs free in ProperPDF?

Yes, on both iPhone and Android. The saved file gets a ProperPDF watermark unless you have Pro.

Are my PDFs uploaded when I merge them?

No. ProperPDF merges the files on your phone and doesn't send them to a server, so it also works in airplane mode.

Does merging lower the quality of my PDF?

No. The pages are copied into the new file without being re-compressed.

Can I merge PDFs on iPhone without an app?

On iOS 26 and later, yes: Apple's Preview app can insert pages from another PDF. See our guide to merging PDFs on iPhone without an app.
