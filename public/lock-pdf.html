Password protect a PDF on your phone

Add a password before you share a PDF with private details in it.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

To password-protect a PDF on your phone, open Lock PDF in ProperPDF, pick the file, enter and confirm a password and save the protected copy. It runs offline on iPhone, iPad and Android. Lock and Unlock are Pro tools. ProperPDF can't recover a forgotten password, so keep the original or store the password safely.

How to lock a PDF in ProperPDF

  1. Open ProperPDF and tap Lock/Unlock PDF.
  2. Select the PDF.
  3. Type a password and confirm it, or tap Generate Strong Password.
  4. Save. ProperPDF writes a protected copy.
  5. Open the copy and test the password before you send it.

Open passwords and restrictions

A full lock means nobody can open the PDF without the password. You can also set restrictions instead, which let people open the file but limit printing or editing in standard PDF viewers. Restrictions are easier to bypass with specialist tools than an open password.

Share the password separately

Send the PDF by email and the password by text message or a call. If both travel in the same thread, the lock does little.

Removing a password

If you know a PDF's password and want an unlocked copy, use Unlock in the same tool.

Questions

Can ProperPDF remove a password I don't know?

No. Unlock needs the current password.

Is locking a PDF free?

No. Lock and Unlock are part of ProperPDF Pro.
