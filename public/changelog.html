CHANGELOG

What's new in ProperPDF

Every release on iPhone, iPad and Android, newest first.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

ProperPDF is updated on both platforms. The iPhone and Android apps are built separately, so a version number can arrive on each at a different time, with different changes.

1.4.2 iPhone, iPad and Android · September 2026

  • Open the ProperPDF website from Settings.
  • Updated the support email and the Privacy Policy and Terms links.

1.4.1 Android · September 2026

  • A new design that matches the iPhone app.
  • A shorter first-run setup.
  • The scanner no longer fails to open on phones without certified Google Play services.

1.4.1 iPhone and iPad · September 2026

  • Performance and stability improvements.

1.4.0 iPhone and iPad · June 2026

  • A redesigned app, from the home screen to every tool.
  • A shorter first-run setup.
  • Text you add in Fill Forms now looks and edits correctly.

1.4.0 Android · May 2026

  • Merge, split and compress handle large PDFs without running out of memory.
  • Split shows a full page preview.
  • Pages render correctly in dark theme.

1.3.0 iPhone and iPad · June 2026

  • AI chat rebuilt on Apple's on-device models, and you can start a chat from the library.
  • Compression no longer makes already-optimized PDFs bigger.

1.3.0 Android · April 2026

  • Place signatures on each page separately.
  • A new first-run setup.
  • Large PDFs no longer run out of memory.

1.2.0 iPhone and iPad · May 2026

  • Redeem App Store offer codes from Settings.

1.2.0 Android · March 2026

  • Organize Pages: reorder, rotate and delete pages.
  • When locking a PDF, you can restrict printing and editing instead of requiring a password to open it.
  • Stability fixes for large files and Image to PDF (1.2.1 and 1.2.2).

1.1.0 iPhone and iPad · May 2026

  • Sign several pages in one go.
  • Five more languages.
  • Runs on iOS 17 and later (previously a newer version was required).
  • Better answers from AI chat.

1.1.0 Android · February 2026

  • Fill forms that have no form fields by placing text on the page.
  • Improved signing.

1.0 iPhone and iPad · April 2026

  • First release: scan, merge, split, compress, organize, sign, fill, lock, convert, edit metadata and on-device AI chat, in six languages.

Android versions before 1.1.0 and minor bug-fix releases are not listed.
