PROPERPDF GUIDE

Offline PDF editor for Android

What ProperPDF can and can't do on Android, what's free, and what needs Pro.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

ProperPDF is an offline PDF app for Android 8.0 and later. It scans, merges, splits, organizes, converts, compresses, signs, fills and password-protects PDFs on the phone, without uploading them and without an account. The scanning, merging, splitting, organizing and conversion tools are free, with a ProperPDF watermark on saved files; Pro removes the watermark and unlocks compress, sign, fill, lock and the on-device AI chat. It doesn't edit the existing text in a PDF.

A typical workflow

Scan the pages with the camera or open a PDF from another app. Merge it with attachments, remove pages with Split or Organize Pages, add a signature and share it from Android's share sheet. Each step saves a new copy, so the original stays available.

What it doesn't do

ProperPDF can't change the existing text in a PDF, convert PDF to Word, or add highlights and comments. Adobe Acrobat Reader and Xodo both annotate for free; text editing in Acrobat is a paid feature. PDFgear is a free option that edits text.

Offline, with one exception

The PDF tools need no connection. The AI chat needs a one-time model download before it works offline.

Questions

Does ProperPDF work offline on Android?

Yes. All PDF tools run on the phone. The AI chat needs a one-time model download first.

Which Android versions does ProperPDF support?

Android 8.0 and later.

Does ProperPDF have ads?

No. The free version adds a watermark to files saved with some tools instead.
