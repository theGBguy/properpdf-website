Questions

Anything else: support.

Do my PDFs get uploaded?

No. ProperPDF processes every file on your phone or tablet. There is no ProperPDF server that receives your documents.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every PDF tool works offline. The on-device AI chat needs a one-time model download on some devices before it works offline.

What's free and what needs Pro?

Scan, merge, split, organize and convert are free. Merge and split add a watermark unless you have Pro, and on Android most free tools do. Compress, sign, fill forms and lock need Pro. Full list.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later.
