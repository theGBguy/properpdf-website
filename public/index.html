GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

More help is available on the support page.

Do my PDFs get uploaded?

ProperPDF processes PDF documents on your device. The website is informational and does not ask you to upload a file.

Does it work without internet?

PDF tools work offline. On-device AI may require a one-time model download on some devices before it can work offline.

Is ProperPDF available on Android and iPhone?

Yes. Download it from the App Store for iPhone and iPad, or from Google Play for Android. Some features and purchase options may vary by platform.

Which tools need Pro?

On iOS, compression, signing, form filling, and password protection are marked as Pro tools. Check the app for the current offer and availability on your device.
