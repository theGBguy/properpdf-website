ProperPDF vs Adobe Acrobat Reader on mobile

Acrobat Reader gives away signing; ProperPDF gives away merging and splitting. The rest depends on what you need.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

Pick Adobe Acrobat Reader if you mainly read, annotate, and fill and sign forms: those are free in Acrobat. Pick ProperPDF if you mainly merge, split, scan and compress, want no account and no ads, and prefer a one-time purchase ($29.99 in the US) to a subscription. Acrobat's paid plans do much more, including editing text and exporting to Word; ProperPDF does neither.

Where Acrobat wins

Annotating, filling forms and signing are free in Acrobat Reader and Pro-only in ProperPDF. Acrobat's paid plans also edit text and images, export to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and sync files through Adobe's cloud. It is by far the more complete PDF editor.

Where ProperPDF wins

Merging, splitting, organizing pages and scanning are free in ProperPDF and paid (or in a separate app) with Acrobat. ProperPDF has no account, no ads, works offline, and offers a one-time purchase.

Questions

Is ProperPDF cheaper than Adobe Acrobat?

ProperPDF Pro is $3.99 a month or $29.99 once in the US, and less in many other countries. We couldn't find Adobe's mobile prices on its website, so compare them in the store listing before buying.

Can ProperPDF edit text like Acrobat?

No. ProperPDF works with pages, signatures, form fields and passwords, not the existing text.
