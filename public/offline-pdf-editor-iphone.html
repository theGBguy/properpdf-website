PROPERPDF GUIDE

Offline PDF editor for iPhone and iPad

What ProperPDF can and can't do on iPhone, what's free, and what needs Pro.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

ProperPDF is an offline PDF app for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later). It scans, merges, splits, organizes, converts, compresses, signs, fills and password-protects PDFs on the device, without uploading them and without an account. Scanning, merging, splitting, organizing, converting and the on-device AI chat are free; compress, sign, fill and lock need Pro. It doesn't edit the existing text in a PDF and doesn't have highlighting or annotation yet.

A typical workflow

Scan the paper pages, or import a PDF into the library. Merge it with any attachments, drop the pages nobody needs with Split or Organize Pages, sign it, then share it from the share sheet. Each step saves a new copy, so the original stays available.

What it doesn't do

ProperPDF can't change the existing text in a PDF, convert PDF to Word, or add highlights, comments and drawings. If you need those, PDF Expert or Adobe Acrobat Reader are better fits. Both annotate for free, and both charge for text editing. On iOS 26 and later, Apple's built-in Preview app can also highlight and draw on PDFs.

Offline, with one exception

The PDF tools need no internet connection at all. The on-device AI chat and summary may need a one-time model download on some devices before it can work offline.

Questions

Does ProperPDF work without internet on iPhone?

Yes. All PDF tools run on the device. The AI feature may need a one-time model download first on some devices.

Is ProperPDF free on iPhone?

The app is free to download. Scan, merge, split, organize, convert and AI chat are free; merge and split add a watermark unless you have Pro. Compress, sign, fill forms and lock need Pro.

Can ProperPDF edit the text inside a PDF?

No. It works with whole pages, signatures, form fields and passwords, not with the existing text.
