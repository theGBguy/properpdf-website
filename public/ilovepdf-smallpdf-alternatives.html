ALTERNATIVES

iLovePDF and Smallpdf alternatives that don't upload your files

Both services process files on their servers. These apps do the work on your phone.

Offline processing · No account required

Updated 22 October 2026

iLovePDF and Smallpdf both process your PDFs on their own servers: Smallpdf says it deletes uploads after one hour, and iLovePDF describes securing files while they are on its servers. If you'd rather not upload a document, use an app that works on the device. ProperPDF (iPhone and Android) merges, splits, compresses and signs offline, PDFgear is free and edits text, and Apple's Preview does the basics on iPhones with iOS 26 or later.

Is uploading to iLovePDF or Smallpdf safe?

For most files, probably yes: both are established services, Smallpdf says it deletes uploads after an hour, and iLovePDF's privacy policy says it doesn't use file contents to train AI models. The question is whether you want a passport scan, payslip or medical letter on someone else's server at all, even briefly. If not, the tools below avoid the upload.

What the online tools do better

Both have larger tool sets than any phone app here: PDF to Word with OCR, editing, translation, desktop apps and cloud storage integrations. iLovePDF also sells a desktop app that it markets for offline use.

ProperPDF

Merge, split, scan, organize and convert for free, with a watermark on some free exports. Compress, sign, fill and lock are Pro. Everything runs on the phone, with no account.

PDFgear

Free, with no sign-up. The apps edit, convert, compress, merge and split; its Android FAQ says these work offline, while the AI assistant needs a connection.

Apple Preview

Built into iPhone from iOS 26: scan, fill, sign, insert pages from another PDF, lock with a password, and export a smaller copy.

Questions

Does Smallpdf store my files?

Smallpdf's Google Play listing says uploaded files are deleted one hour after processing unless you save them.

Is there an offline version of iLovePDF?

iLovePDF markets its desktop app for offline use. On a phone, ProperPDF and PDFgear work offline.

What's the cheapest way to compress PDFs without uploading?

PDFgear is free. ProperPDF includes compression in Pro, from $3.99 a month or $29.99 once in the US, with lower regional prices.
